# ARC Overview

## What is ARC?

ARC is a stablecoin-first blockchain ecosystem developed by Circle. Instead of treating stablecoins as just another token, ARC is designed around native stablecoin infrastructure to support payments, financial applications, and onchain commerce.

---

## Core Ideas

- Stablecoin-first architecture
- Native USDC support
- Fast and efficient transactions
- Developer-friendly tooling
- Cross-chain interoperability

---

## Why ARC?

Traditional blockchains were not designed with stablecoins at their core. ARC changes this approach by making stablecoins the foundation of the network, allowing developers to build financial applications with a simpler and more consistent user experience.

---

## Learning Notes

While reading the documentation, one key takeaway is that ARC focuses on improving developer experience. Features such as App Kit, native USDC integration, and built-in infrastructure reduce development complexity and help teams launch applications faster.
